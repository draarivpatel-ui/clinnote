AI Transparency, Privacy Architecture & Clinical Disclaimer

100% Client-Side Privacy Architecture (Zero PHI Transmission): ClinNote executes its entire 38-term clinical NLP de-jargonizer, Flesch-Kincaid readability engine, 24-hour dosing timeline, and pharmacokinetic collision matrix locally inside your browser using pure JavaScript ES modules. A strict Content-Security-Policy with connect-src 'none' guarantees that zero patient health information (PHI) or custom discharge text is ever transmitted to any external server or cloud API.
